ENGLISH VOCABULARY: VERB FORMS V1, V2 AND V3
=============================================

V1 = Base form / Present form
V2 = Simple Past form
V3 = Past Participle form

Examples:

V1: work
V2: worked
V3: worked

V1: go
V2: went
V3: gone


WHERE TO USE V1, V2 AND V3
==========================

Use V1:
- Simple Present: I work every day.
- After do/does/did: Did you work yesterday?
- After will: I will work tomorrow.
- After modal verbs: I can work today.
- After “to”: I want to work.

Use V2:
- Simple Past: I worked yesterday.

Use V3:
- Present Perfect: I have worked today.
- Past Perfect: I had worked before the meeting.
- Future Perfect: I will have worked for eight hours.
- Passive Voice: The issue was resolved.

EXAMPLES WITH V1, V2 AND V3
===========================

1. UPDATE

V1:
I update the website every day.
मैं हर दिन वेबसाइट अपडेट करता हूँ।

V2:
I updated the website yesterday.
मैंने कल वेबसाइट अपडेट की।

V3:
I have updated the website.
मैंने वेबसाइट अपडेट कर दी है।


2. WRITE

V1:
I write professional messages.
मैं professional messages लिखता हूँ।

V2:
I wrote a message yesterday.
मैंने कल एक message लिखा।

V3:
I have written the message.
मैंने message लिख दिया है।


3. GO

V1:
I go to the office every day.
मैं हर दिन office जाता हूँ।

V2:
I went to the office yesterday.
मैं कल office गया था।

V3:
I have gone to the office.
मैं office जा चुका हूँ।


4. SEE

V1:
I see the problem.
मुझे problem दिखाई देती है।

V2:
I saw the problem yesterday.
मैंने कल problem देखी।

V3:
I have seen the problem.
मैं problem देख चुका हूँ।


5. DO

V1:
I do my work carefully.
मैं अपना काम ध्यान से करता हूँ।

V2:
I did the work yesterday.
मैंने कल काम किया।

V3:
I have done the work.
मैंने काम कर दिया है।

COMMON MISTAKES
===============

Incorrect:
I did not updated the page.

Correct:
I did not update the page.

Reason:
“Did” के बाद V1 आता है।


Incorrect:
I have wrote the message.

Correct:
I have written the message.

Reason:
“Have” के बाद V3 आता है।


Incorrect:
I have went to the office.

Correct:
I have gone to the office.


Incorrect:
I will updated the website.

Correct:
I will update the website.

Reason:
“Will” के बाद V1 आता है।


Incorrect:
She have completed the work.

Correct:
She has completed the work.


PRACTICE EXERCISE
=================

Fill in the blanks:

1. I ______ the website every day. (check)
2. I ______ the website yesterday. (check)
3. I have ______ the website. (check)
4. She ______ a message yesterday. (write)
5. She has ______ the message. (write)
6. We ______ to the meeting yesterday. (go)
7. We have ______ to the meeting. (go)
8. Did you ______ the issue? (fix)
9. I will ______ the update tomorrow. (send)
10. He has ______ the product. (choose)

Answers:

1. check
2. checked
3. checked
4. wrote
5. written
6. went
7. gone
8. fix
9. send
10. chosen
