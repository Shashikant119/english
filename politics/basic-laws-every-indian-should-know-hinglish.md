# Har Indian Ko Kaun-Se Basic Laws aur Legal Rights Pata Hone Chahiye?

> **Simple rule:** Har Act aur section yaad hona zaroori nahi. Lekin emergency mein apna basic right, correct authority aur proof preserve karne ka tarika pata hona chahiye.

## 1. Constitution ke basic rights

Har citizen ko kam se kam in constitutional protections ka basic idea hona chahiye:

- **Article 14:** Law ke saamne equality aur equal protection of laws.
- **Article 19:** Citizens ko speech and expression, peaceful assembly, association, movement, residence aur lawful profession/business jaise rights deta hai—subject to constitutional restrictions.
- **Article 20:** Criminal conviction se related protections, including ex post facto punishment, double jeopardy aur self-incrimination se protection.
- **Article 21:** Life aur personal liberty ko procedure established by law ke bina deprive nahi kiya ja sakta.
- **Article 22:** Arrest aur detention ke kuch safeguards deta hai.
- **Article 32 aur 226:** Fundamental/legal rights enforce karne ke liye Supreme Court ya High Court approach kiya ja sakta hai, facts aur remedy ke according.

Freedom of speech ki detailed Hinglish explanation: [Freedom of Speech Note](./freedom-of-speech-constitution-hinglish.md)

## 2. FIR aur police complaint ka basic rule

**Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)** 1 July 2024 se criminal procedure ka main central law hai.

- Cognizable offence ki information police station ko orally ya electronically di ja sakti hai.
- Offence kisi doosre area mein hua ho, tab bhi information di ja sakti hai; is practice ko commonly **Zero FIR** kaha jata hai.
- Oral information ko writing mein convert karke informant ko read over kiya jana chahiye.
- Recorded information/FIR ki copy informant ya victim ko **free of cost** milni chahiye.
- Electronic information ko prescribed process ke according three days ke andar sign karna hota hai.
- SHO information record karne se refuse kare, toh BNSS Section 173(4) ke process ke under written substance concerned Superintendent of Police ko post se bheja ja sakta hai; uske baad Magistrate ka remedy bhi available ho sakta hai.

**Practical habit:** Complaint ki copy, receiving/diary number, email, postal receipt, screenshots aur original files safe rakho.

## 3. Arrest hone par basic rights

Arrest ka matlab automatically guilt prove hona nahi hai. Arrested person ko generally:

- arrest ke grounds bataye jane chahiye;
- bailable offence mein bail ke right ki information milni chahiye;
- relative, friend ya nominated person ko arrest aur place of detention ki information dilwane ka safeguard hota hai;
- interrogation ke dauran apni choice ke advocate se milne ka right hota hai, though throughout interrogation continuously present rehne ka general right nahi;
- unnecessary restraint nahi lagaya jana chahiye;
- Constitution ke Article 22(2) ke according, journey time exclude karke 24 hours ke andar nearest Magistrate ke saamne produce kiya jana chahiye; Magistrate ki authority ke bina usse zyada custody nahi.

Women ki arrest ke liye additional safeguards hain. BNSS ke under, exceptional circumstances ko chhodkar woman ko sunset ke baad aur sunrise se pehle arrest nahi kiya jana chahiye; exception ke liye woman police officer ko written report ke through Judicial Magistrate First Class ki prior permission leni hoti hai.

**Never do:** Police se physically resist, evidence destroy ya false information provide mat karo. Calm raho, lawyer/family ko inform karo aur documents bina samjhe sign mat karo.

## 4. Police questioning aur documents

- Witness, complainant aur accused ke rights same nahi hote; apni legal capacity samajhna important hai.
- Blank paper ya unread statement sign mat karo.
- Kisi notice, seizure memo, arrest memo ya acknowledgement ki copy maango aur safely rakho.
- Phone/laptop/search/seizure ka exact legal answer facts aur applicable law par depend karta hai; dispute ho toh confrontation ke bajay objection record karao aur advocate se advice lo.

## 5. Cyber fraud aur online safety

Agar UPI, card, banking ya online financial fraud ho:

1. **Immediately 1930 call karo.**
2. [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/) par complaint file karo.
3. Bank/payment app ko turant inform karke transaction block/freeze request karo.
4. Transaction ID, account number, phone number, chat, email, URL aur screenshots preserve karo.
5. OTP, PIN, CVV, password ya remote-screen access kisi ko mat do—even if caller police, bank ya government officer hone ka claim kare.

Fast reporting important hai; delay se funds trace ya hold karna difficult ho sakta hai.

## 6. Consumer rights

**Consumer Protection Act, 2019** goods, services, e-commerce aur unfair trade practices se related protection provide karta hai.

- Bill/invoice, warranty, packaging, order confirmation aur payment proof preserve karo.
- Seller/service provider ko written complaint do.
- Resolution na mile toh **National Consumer Helpline 1915**, [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/) ya appropriate Consumer Commission ka option dekho.
- Misleading advertisement, defective goods, deficient service aur unfair practices ke against remedy facts ke according available ho sakti hai.

Har bad experience automatically legal violation nahi hota; warranty terms, evidence, limitation aur jurisdiction matter karte hain.

## 7. RTI ka right

**Right to Information Act, 2005** ke through Indian citizen public authority ke paas existing records/information maang sakta hai.

- RTI questions ya explanations demand karne ke bajay existing documents, records, file noting, orders ya data clearly identify karo.
- Central Government public authorities ke liye [RTI Online](https://rtionline.gov.in/) portal use kiya ja sakta hai.
- State authorities ke liye respective State RTI process/portal use hota hai; Central portal par State RTI file nahi karni chahiye.
- Ordinary cases mein response ki statutory timeline generally 30 days hoti hai; life or liberty matters aur kuch special situations mein different timelines apply hoti hain.
- No response ya unsatisfactory response par first appeal aur phir applicable second-appeal/complaint mechanism available hai.

RTI grievance redressal ka replacement nahi; iska main purpose records/information access karna hai.

## 8. Workplace par important rules

- Appointment letter, salary slips, attendance, appraisal, resignation aur work-related communication ki personal copies lawfully preserve karo.
- Salary, notice period, leave, termination aur benefits ke rules employment category, contract, State aur applicable labour law par depend karte hain.
- Women ke workplace sexual harassment complaints ke liye **POSH Act, 2013** protection aur redressal framework deta hai.
- Covered workplace mein Internal Committee ka mechanism ho sakta hai; certain cases mein Local Committee relevant hoti hai.
- Retaliation, threat ya harassment ho toh dates, messages, witnesses aur complaints ka factual record maintain karo.

Specific employment dispute mein applicable State law aur employment status verify karna zaroori hai.

## 9. Women, children aur vulnerable persons ki protection

- Immediate danger mein **112** emergency service contact karo.
- Child sexual abuse se related matters mein **POCSO Act** applicable ho sakta hai; child ki identity aur privacy protect karna important hai.
- Domestic violence sirf physical violence tak limited nahi ho sakti; applicable law mein sexual, verbal/emotional aur economic abuse bhi relevant ho sakte hain.
- Women, children, SC/ST members, persons with disability, persons in custody aur other eligible categories ko free legal aid mil sakti hai.
- NALSA legal-aid helpline: **15100**.

## 10. Road aur accident situation

- Valid driving licence, registration, insurance, pollution certificate aur applicable permits maintain karo.
- Accident mein pehle safety aur medical help prioritize karo; **112** call karo when required.
- Scene ko unsafe banaye bina photos/video, vehicle number, witness details aur insurance information record karo.
- Injured person ki help karne wale Good Samaritan ke liye legal protections/guidelines hain; unnecessary fear ki wajah se emergency help delay mat karo.
- Police/insurance ko facts honestly report karo; fabricated version ya fake evidence serious problem create kar sakta hai.

## 11. Property, rent aur money transactions

- Cash deal ke bajay traceable payment aur written receipt prefer karo.
- Rent, security deposit, maintenance, notice period aur inventory ko written agreement mein record karo.
- Property buy karne se pehle title, encumbrance, approvals, tax dues, possession aur registration independently verify karao.
- Blank cheque, blank stamp paper, blank signed page ya OTP hand over mat karo.
- Loan dene ya partnership start karne mein amount, purpose, repayment, ownership, authority aur exit terms writing mein rakho.

Property aur tenancy laws State-specific ho sakte hain; large transaction se pehle local property lawyer se title due diligence karao.

## 12. Evidence preserve karne ka golden rule

Legal dispute mein “maine bola tha” se zyada useful contemporaneous evidence hota hai:

- original bill, agreement aur receipt;
- full email/chat export, sirf selected screenshot nahi;
- date, time, phone number, URL aur transaction ID;
- complaint acknowledgement and postal tracking;
- unedited photo/video ki original file;
- medical report aur expense records;
- chronological incident notes.

Evidence edit, fabricate, threaten, publicly leak ya illegally obtain mat karo. Original ko backup karke working copy use karo.

## Emergency checklist

| Situation | First practical step |
|---|---|
| Immediate physical danger | Call **112** |
| Cyber financial fraud | Call **1930** and report at cybercrime.gov.in |
| Consumer complaint | Keep invoice; contact seller, then **1915/NCH** |
| Need free legal help | Call **NALSA 15100** or contact DLSA/SLSA |
| Cognizable offence | Police complaint/FIR; obtain free copy |
| Government record needed | File RTI with correct public authority |

## Final takeaway

Law jaanne ka best formula:

**Right samjho + written proof rakho + deadline miss mat karo + correct authority approach karo + serious matter mein qualified lawyer lo.**

## Important disclaimer

Yeh general legal-awareness note hai, legal advice nahi. Laws amend ho sakte hain aur result facts, State rules, evidence, limitation aur court decisions par depend karta hai. Arrest, criminal complaint, property, employment termination, domestic violence ya large financial dispute mein promptly qualified advocate se case-specific advice lo.
