# UPSC Civil Services Examination: Complete A-to-Z Roadmap (Hinglish)

> Yeh master note **UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE)**—IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS aur allied civil services—ke liye hai. Isse padhne ke baad beginner ko exam structure, subjects, books, strategy, optional, timetable, answer writing, revision, tests aur interview ka complete direction mil jana chahiye.

> **Important:** Rules, dates, vacancies, eligibility aur application process har saal change ho sakte hain. Apne attempt ki official UPSC notification final authority hai. Is note ka exam structure official CSE 2026 notification se verify kiya gaya hai.

---

## 1. UPSC CSE exactly kya hai?

UPSC CSE ek single exam nahi, ek long selection cycle hai:

1. **Preliminary Examination:** Objective screening test.
2. **Main Written Examination:** Descriptive papers.
3. **Interview/Personality Test:** Personality aur suitability assessment.
4. **Final rank:** Mains merit papers + Interview ke marks se.
5. **Service allocation:** Rank, category, service preferences, vacancies, eligibility/medical standards aur applicable government rules ke basis par.

Prelims ke marks final merit mein add nahi hote. Prelims sirf Mains ke liye qualify karata hai.

---

## 2. Eligibility ka basic idea

### Education

Recognised university ki **graduate degree** ya equivalent qualification required hoti hai. Stream matter nahi karti—BA, BSc, BCom, BTech, MBBS ya kisi recognised discipline ka graduate apply kar sakta hai. Final-stage candidates ke liye notification mein provisional conditions hoti hain.

### Age

CSE 2026 mein general rule: candidate ko 1 August 2026 ko **21 years complete** aur **32 years se kam** hona tha. Reserved/specified categories ko notification ke according relaxation mil sakti hai.

### Attempts

CSE 2026 notification ke according:

- General/EWS: 6 attempts;
- OBC: 9 attempts;
- SC/ST: age eligibility ke andar unlimited;
- PwBD: category ke according specified relaxation.

Prelims ke kisi ek paper mein actually appear karna attempt count kara sakta hai. Sirf form fill karke absent rehna generally attempt nahi hota. Exact category rules current notification se verify karo.

### Nationality

IAS, IFS aur IPS ke liye Indian citizen hona required hai. Other services ke nationality provisions wider ho sakte hain, subject to official eligibility certificate conditions.

### First action

Preparation start karne se pehle official notification mein yeh verify karo:

- date of birth and age relaxation;
- graduation/equivalent status;
- category certificate format and validity;
- number of used attempts;
- nationality;
- PwBD/scribe rules;
- desired services ke medical/physical standards.

## 3. Exam pattern and marks

### Stage 1: Prelims

| Paper | Marks | Duration | Nature |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| General Studies Paper I | 200 | 2 hours | Cut-off deciding paper |
| General Studies Paper II (CSAT) | 200 | 2 hours | Qualifying; minimum 33% |

- Both papers objective MCQ hain.
- Wrong answer par assigned marks ka **one-third negative marking** hota hai.
- Blank answer par penalty nahi.
- Dono papers mein appear karna mandatory hai.
- CSAT qualify karna aur GS-I cut-off clear karna dono necessary hain.

### Stage 2: Mains Written

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Paper A | One Indian Language | 300 | Qualifying |
| Paper B | English | 300 | Qualifying |
| Paper I | Essay | 250 | Merit |
| Paper II | GS I | 250 | Merit |
| Paper III | GS II | 250 | Merit |
| Paper IV | GS III | 250 | Merit |
| Paper V | GS IV | 250 | Merit |
| Paper VI | Optional Paper I | 250 | Merit |
| Paper VII | Optional Paper II | 250 | Merit |

Written merit total: **1750 marks**.

Qualifying Indian Language aur English mein official minimum standard meet karna compulsory hai; otherwise merit papers ka result count nahi hoga. 2026 rules mein minimum qualifying standard 25% each stated tha.

### Stage 3: Interview

- Personality Test: **275 marks**.
- Officially no fixed minimum qualifying marks.
- Final total: **1750 + 275 = 2025 marks**.

## 4. Preparation ka master principle

UPSC preparation ko 7 connected parts samjho:

1. **Official syllabus**—boundary batata hai.
2. **Previous Year Questions (PYQs)**—UPSC kya aur kaise poochta hai.
3. **Limited standard sources**—content foundation.
4. **Current affairs**—static topics ka contemporary application.
5. **Revision**—knowledge retain karata hai.
6. **Practice/tests**—knowledge ko marks mein convert karta hai.
7. **Feedback and correction**—repeat mistakes rokta hai.

Formula:

**Syllabus → PYQ → Source → Short notes → Revision → Questions → Error analysis → Re-revision**

Sirf books collect karna preparation nahi. Ek source ko multiple times revise karna usually multiple sources ko once read karne se better hai.

## 6. Prelims GS-I: complete subject list

Official syllabus broadly yeh cover karta hai:

- current events of national and international importance;
- History of India and Indian National Movement;
- Indian and World Geography;
- Indian Polity and Governance;
- Economic and Social Development;
- Environmental Ecology, Biodiversity and Climate Change;
- General Science.

Preparation ke liye ise practical modules mein break karo.

### A. Polity and Governance

Read:

- Constitution: historical background, making, features, Preamble;
- Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Fundamental Duties;
- President, Vice-President, PM, Council of Ministers;
- Parliament and parliamentary procedures;
- Supreme Court, High Courts, judicial review;
- Centre–State relations, federalism, emergency;
- constitutional and statutory bodies;
- Panchayati Raj and municipalities;
- elections, anti-defection, political parties;
- governance, transparency, rights issues and major constitutional developments.

Core sources:

- NCERT political-science books for basics;
- M. Laxmikanth, *Indian Polity*;
- selected constitutional Articles/bare text;
- PIB/PRS and reliable newspaper for relevant developments.

Do not: Har Article ratta mat lagao. Institution ka purpose, powers, appointment, removal, independence aur comparison samjho.

### B. Modern History and National Movement

Read:

- European entry and British expansion;
- economic impact of colonial rule;
- socio-religious reform movements;
- Revolt of 1857;
- early associations and formation of INC;
- Moderates, Extremists and revolutionary movements;
- Gandhian phase and mass movements;
- constitutional developments;
- communal politics, partition and independence;
- important personalities, organisations, newspapers and sessions.

Core sources:

- NCERT/old NCERT foundation;
- Spectrum, *A Brief History of Modern India*;
- PYQ-based timelines and revision sheets.

### C. Ancient and Medieval History

Read selectively but systematically:

- Indus Valley, Vedic period, Buddhism and Jainism;
- Maurya, post-Maurya and Gupta themes;
- Sangam age and major southern kingdoms;
- temple architecture, literature, philosophy and religion;
- Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Bhakti-Sufi traditions;
- Mughal administration, society, culture and economy.

Sources:

- relevant NCERTs;
- one concise standard source;
- culture topics ke saath integrated notes.

### D. Art and Culture

Read:

- architecture and sculpture;
- painting traditions;
- classical and folk dance/music;
- literature and philosophy;
- religion, schools and cultural institutions;
- UNESCO heritage and current-linked cultural news.

Sources:

- Class 11 *An Introduction to Indian Art*;
- CCRT selected material;
- Nitin Singhania selectively, syllabus/PYQ ke according.

### E. Geography

Read:

- geomorphology, climatology and oceanography;
- latitude/longitude, seasons and time zones;
- earthquakes, volcanoes, rocks and landforms;
- atmosphere, winds, rainfall, cyclones and ocean currents;
- Indian physiography, rivers, climate, soils and vegetation;
- agriculture, resources, industries and population;
- world map locations and places in news.

Sources:

- NCERT Class 6–12 relevant geography books;
- G.C. Leong for physical geography;
- a reliable atlas;
- daily map practice.

### F. Economy

Read concepts first:

- GDP/GVA, growth, inflation and unemployment;
- fiscal policy, budget, taxation and public debt;
- monetary policy, RBI and banking;
- balance of payments, exchange rate and trade;
- poverty, inequality, inclusion and demographics;
- agriculture economics, MSP, procurement and food security;
- infrastructure and investment;
- external sector and international economic institutions;
- government schemes only with purpose, beneficiaries, ministry and design.

Sources:

- NCERT economics foundation;
- one standard Indian Economy book/course notes;
- latest Economic Survey and Union Budget summaries plus important original chapters;
- RBI and ministry explainers where relevant.

### G. Environment and Ecology

Read:

- ecosystem, food chain/web and ecological succession;
- biodiversity, species categories and protected areas;
- pollution and environmental impact assessment;
- climate change science, mitigation and adaptation;
- international conventions and organisations;
- Indian environmental institutions and laws at overview level;
- species/locations in current affairs.

Sources:

- NCERT biology ecology chapters;
- one standard environment source;
- Ministry of Environment and treaty websites for updates;
- map-based protected-area revision.

### H. Science and Technology

Focus on application, not engineering-level detail:

- space missions and satellite applications;
- biotechnology, genetics, vaccines and health technology;
- AI, quantum, semiconductors, robotics and cybersecurity;
- defence and nuclear technology basics;
- energy technologies;
- everyday general science connected with news.

Sources:

- school-level science basics where weak;
- newspaper/current-affairs notes;
- official ISRO, DST, DBT, MeitY and ministry explainers.

### I. Current Affairs

Current affairs separate subject nahi; har static subject ka current layer hai.

Cover:

- major national policies and laws;
- Supreme Court/constitutional developments;
- economy, budget and reports;
- international relations and organisations;
- environment, species and conventions;
- science and technology;
- social issues, health and education;
- important places in news.

Avoid:

- celebrity news;
- every political statement;
- daily crime details without policy relevance;
- endless monthly-magazine accumulation.

## 7. CSAT ko qualifying bolkar ignore mat karo

CSAT official syllabus:

- comprehension;
- communication/interpersonal skills;
- logical reasoning and analytical ability;
- decision making and problem solving;
- general mental ability;
- Class X level numeracy;
- data interpretation and data sufficiency.

Minimum 33% means 200 mein at least 66 marks ka official threshold, but exam-day safety ke liye mocks mein consistent buffer target karo.

### CSAT diagnostic rule

Preparation ke first week mein latest/full PYQ timed solve karo:

- **Strong:** Weekly one practice session + regular mocks.
- **Average:** 3 sessions/week, topic repair + PYQs.
- **Weak:** Daily 60–90 minutes until stable qualifying score.

Reading comprehension, basic maths ya reasoning weakness ko Prelims ke last month tak postpone mat karo.

## 11. Mains GS-IV: Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude

Prepare:

- ethics and human interface;
- human values and lessons from leaders/reformers;
- attitude and behaviour;
- aptitude and civil-service foundational values;
- emotional intelligence;
- moral thinkers and philosophers;
- ethics in public administration;
- accountability and ethical governance;
- probity, RTI, codes, citizen charter, work culture and corruption;
- case studies.

Create personal examples bank:

- integrity;
- empathy;
- courage;
- impartiality;
- accountability;
- conflict of interest;
- public-service motivation;
- failure and learning.

Case-study framework:

1. Facts and ethical issues.
2. Stakeholders.
3. Values/duties in conflict.
4. Available options.
5. Each option ke pros/cons.
6. Chosen action with legal, ethical and practical justification.
7. Implementation and safeguards.

Do not write unrealistic heroism. Lawful, humane, transparent aur implementable solution do.

## 12. Essay paper ka complete approach

Essay GS answer ka long version nahi. Ismein theme understanding, coherence, originality, balance aur expression test hota hai.

### Weekly practice

- Week 1–4: outlines and introductions.
- Week 5 onward: one full essay every 1–2 weeks.
- Exam se pehle timed full-paper simulations.

### Essay structure

- engaging but relevant introduction;
- theme/keyword definition;
- multiple dimensions: historical, social, political, economic, ethical, scientific, environmental, international and personal;
- arguments and counter-arguments;
- examples/data/anecdotes;
- constructive way forward;
- conclusion jo central theme par return kare.

Avoid:

- forced quotations;
- unrelated data dumping;
- extreme one-sided claims;
- headings ka excessive use if flow break hota ho;
- memorised essay ko topic par force karna.

## 13. Optional subject kaise choose karein?

Optional total **500 marks** ka hai, isliye random trend dekhkar select mat karo.

Official optionals include Agriculture, Anthropology, Botany, Chemistry, Civil Engineering, Commerce & Accountancy, Economics, Electrical Engineering, Geography, Geology, History, Law, Management, Mathematics, Mechanical Engineering, Medical Science, Philosophy, Physics, PSIR, Psychology, Public Administration, Sociology, Statistics, Zoology and listed language literatures.

### Selection matrix

Har shortlisted subject ko 1–5 score do:

| Factor | Question |
|---|---|
| Interest | Kya 6–12 months same subject padh sakta hoon? |
| Syllabus comfort | Topics understandable aur manageable lag rahe hain? |
| PYQ comfort | Questions dekhkar curiosity aati hai ya complete disconnect? |
| Material | Reliable sources, PYQs and guidance available hain? |
| Answer aptitude | Technical, analytical ya humanities style suit karta hai? |
| Background | Graduation knowledge genuine advantage deta hai? |
| Time | Syllabus realistic time mein cover/revise ho sakta hai? |
| Overlap | GS/Essay overlap bonus hai, but deciding factor nahi. |

### 7-day optional trial

1. Official syllabus print karo.
2. 5–10 years PYQs classify karo.
3. Har shortlisted optional ka one representative topic padho.
4. Do answers likho.
5. Material availability verify karo.
6. Successful candidates ki strategies sirf context ke liye dekho.
7. Decision lock karke unnecessary switching band karo.

“Most scoring optional” permanently exist nahi karta. Best optional woh hai jisme tum syllabus complete, revise aur quality answers reproduce kar sako.

## 16. Newspaper aur current affairs ka correct method

Daily 60–90 minutes sufficient ho sakte hain after learning selection.

### Read

- explained policy issue;
- constitutional/judicial development;
- economy and government data;
- international relations;
- environment and S&T;
- social-sector challenges;
- editorial mein balanced arguments.

### Skip/skim

- routine political allegations;
- local crime details;
- sports/entertainment unless syllabus linkage;
- duplicate event reporting;
- facts with no likely analytical or MCQ value.

### Issue-note template

Har important issue ko one page mein:

- What/why in news?
- Background.
- Syllabus tags.
- Key facts/data.
- Stakeholders.
- Benefits/opportunities.
- Problems/criticism.
- Constitutional/institutional angle.
- Way forward.
- Prelims facts.
- Mains examples.

Daily news notes ko monthly revise aur consolidate karo. Otherwise notes khud backlog ban jayenge.

## 17. Notes kaise banayein?

### Three-layer system

1. **Base notes:** First complete understanding; moderate detail.
2. **Revision notes:** Har syllabus keyword par 1–3 pages.
3. **Last-week sheets:** Facts, maps, diagrams, committees, examples, formulas and recurring mistakes.

### Notes kab banayein?

- First reading: underline lightly, big notes mat banao.
- Second reading + PYQ understanding: consolidated notes banao.
- Tests ke baad: missing points/error log add karo.

### Digital vs handwritten

Dono valid hain. Choose based on speed and revision comfort:

- Digital: searchable, easily updated.
- Handwritten: memory and exam-handwriting benefit.
- Hybrid: static handwritten + current digital also works.

Tool change karte rehna productive work nahi.

## 18. Revision system jo actually kaam kare

Suggested spaced cycle:

- same day: 10-minute recall;
- after 2–3 days: quick revision;
- after 7 days: active recall + questions;
- after 21–30 days: compressed revision;
- before exam: last-week sheet.

Active recall methods:

- book close karke headings reproduce karo;
- blank map fill karo;
- flashcards for facts;
- 10 MCQs/2 Mains questions solve karo;
- topic kisi aur ko explain karo;
- syllabus line dekhkar content framework recall karo.

Reading without recall familiarity deta hai, mastery nahi.

## 19. Prelims question practice and test strategy

### PYQ first

Last 10–15 years Prelims questions subject-wise analyse karo:

- knowledge-based;
- conceptual;
- statement/elimination based;
- current-static linkage;
- map/species/institution based.

### MCQ progression

- Foundation phase: 20–30 quality questions/day.
- Consolidation: subject tests.
- Prelims phase: mixed full-length tests under time.

### Error log categories

- fact not known;
- concept unclear;
- question misread;
- two options confusion;
- overthinking;
- risky guess;
- time pressure;
- marked wrong bubble.

Score se zyada error pattern important hai.

### Attempt strategy

Universal magic attempt number nahi. Apni accuracy data se decide karo:

- Round 1: sure questions.
- Round 2: reasoned elimination.
- Round 3: only calculated risk.
- Blind guesses avoid karo because one-third negative marking.

OMR practice mandatory hai.

## 20. Mains answer writing from zero

Answer writing syllabus ka 60–70% complete hone ka wait mat karo. Basic foundation ke baad start karo.

### Decode directive

- **Discuss:** Multiple dimensions with reasoned conclusion.
- **Examine:** Claim/topic ko inspect with evidence.
- **Critically examine:** Strengths + limitations + judgment.
- **Analyse:** Components and relationships.
- **Evaluate:** Criteria-based assessment.
- **Comment:** Informed position with justification.
- **Elucidate/Explain:** Clear detailed clarification.

### Basic answer structure

1. **Introduction:** Definition, context, data, constitutional provision or recent trigger.
2. **Body:** Subheadings according to demand; balanced dimensions.
3. **Way forward:** Specific, feasible and institutional.
4. **Conclusion:** Positive, constitutional and future-oriented.

### Value addition

- relevant Articles/judgments;
- committee/reports;
- credible data;
- examples and case studies;
- maps, flowcharts and small diagrams;
- SDGs and constitutional values;
- best practices.

Diagram tabhi use karo jab clarity improve kare. Decoration marks guarantee nahi karta.

### Practice progression

- Week 1–2: untimed one answer/day.
- Week 3–6: 2–3 timed answers/day.
- Later: sectional and full-length tests.
- Every test: self-review/model comparison/mentor feedback + rewrite weak answers.

## 26. Interview/Personality Test preparation

Interview knowledge quiz alone nahi. Board suitability, judgment, clarity, balance, honesty and awareness assess karta hai.

Prepare:

- Detailed Application Form (DAF) ka every word;
- name, birthplace, district and state;
- graduation subject;
- job/work experience;
- hobbies and achievements;
- service and cadre preferences;
- current national/international issues;
- situational and ethical questions;
- why civil services?

Response style:

- question patiently listen;
- direct answer first;
- balanced reasoning;
- fact nahi pata toh honestly accept;
- bluff, aggression and artificial jargon avoid;
- respectful disagreement allowed.

Mocks useful hain, but too many mocks personality ko scripted bana sakte hain. Feedback patterns correct karo, personality imitate mat karo.

## 29. Coaching necessary hai?

**No, compulsory nahi.** Coaching structure, content and feedback provide kar sakti hai; selection guarantee nahi.

Coaching useful ho sakti hai if:

- self-structure maintain nahi hota;
- optional guidance genuinely unavailable;
- answer feedback needed;
- basics bahut weak hain.

Self-study sufficient ho sakta hai if:

- official syllabus/PYQ samajhkar plan bana sakte ho;
- limited sources follow karte ho;
- tests and feedback arrange kar sakte ho;
- discipline consistent hai.

Paid material lene se pehle free official sources and sample quality evaluate karo. FOMO-driven subscriptions avoid karo.

## 31. Frequently asked doubts

### Kya NCERT Class 6–12 sab line-by-line padhni hai?

Relevant books and chapters padho. Weak foundation mein broader reading useful; limited time mein syllabus/PYQ-based selection karo.

### Current affairs kitne months ki?

Fixed magic number nahi. Exam ke preceding period ka comprehensive coverage zaroori hai, but issues often multi-year background demand karte hain. One newspaper + monthly consolidation + revision is safer than arbitrary month counting.

### Kya daily 12–14 hours necessary hain?

Nahi. Consistent effective hours, recall, tests and revision matter. Unsustainable hours burnout create karte hain.

### Kya first attempt mein clear ho sakta hai?

Haan, possible hai; guaranteed nahi. Attempt number se zyada preparation quality matters.

### Graduation subject hi optional lena chahiye?

Only if interest, syllabus, PYQs and answer aptitude align karein. Background advantage compulsory choice nahi.

### Kya notes banana compulsory hai?

Revision system compulsory hai. Notes uska common tool hain; book annotations + digital sheets bhi work kar sakte hain.

### Prelims ke baad Mains start karunga?

Risky. Optional, GS understanding, Essay and answer writing ka base Prelims se pehle ready hona chahiye.

### Kya facts ya analysis zyada important?

Prelims mein accurate facts + concepts + elimination. Mains mein relevant facts supported analysis. Dono ka balance chahiye.

### Test series compulsory?

Brand compulsory nahi, timed testing compulsory hai. Quality questions, honest simulation and error correction arrange karo.

### Low mock marks aaye toh attempt chhod doon?

Single score par decision mat lo. Multiple recent full mocks, PYQ performance, CSAT safety, revision level and error trend analyse karo.

## 34. One-page strategy summary

If poora note bhool jao, yeh sequence yaad rakho:

1. Official notification and syllabus read karo.
2. PYQs analyse karo.
3. Optional early select karo.
4. Limited sources se foundation complete karo.
5. Current affairs ko static syllabus se link karo.
6. Short revision notes banao.
7. CSAT regularly practise karo.
8. MCQs aur Mains answers early start karo.
9. Multiple revisions and timed tests karo.
10. Har error ka reason record aur correct karo.
11. Prelims ke baad result ka wait kiye bina Mains mode.
12. Interview mein informed, balanced and honest raho.

**UPSC ka secret hidden book nahi hai. Clear syllabus, limited sources, repeated revision, quality practice, feedback aur long-term consistency hi core system hai.**
