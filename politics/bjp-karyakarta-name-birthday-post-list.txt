============================================================

1. Narendra Modi is the Prime Minister of India.
2. The BJP National President leads the party organisation.
3. A Chief Minister leads the state government.
4. An MP represents people in Parliament.
5. A karyakarta works at ground level for the party.
6. Booth-level workers connect directly with voters.
7. State leaders manage political work in their states.
8. National spokespersons explain party views to the media.
