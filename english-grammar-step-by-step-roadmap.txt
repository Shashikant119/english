ENGLISH GRAMMAR: STEP-BY-STEP LEARNING ROADMAP
==============================================

इस guide को beginner से advanced क्रम में बनाया गया है।
हर step पूरा करने के बाद ही अगले step पर जाएँ।


HOW TO STUDY EVERY DAY
======================

रोज 30–45 minutes इस तरह practice करें:

1. 10 minutes: नया grammar rule समझें।
2. 10 minutes: दिए गए examples पढ़ें और बोलें।
3. 10 minutes: अपने 5–10 sentences लिखें।
4. 5 minutes: sentences को negative और question में बदलें।
5. 5 minutes: अपनी mistakes check करें।

Golden rule:
केवल rules याद न करें। हर rule से अपने daily work के sentences बनाएँ।

Example:
- I update the website.
- I am updating the website.
- I updated the website yesterday.
- I will update the website tomorrow.

STEP 1: SENTENCE BASICS
=======================

पहले समझें कि एक basic English sentence कैसे बनता है।

Basic structure:
Subject + Verb + Object

Subject:
काम करने वाला व्यक्ति या वस्तु।

Verb:
Action या state बताने वाला word।

Object:
जिस व्यक्ति या वस्तु पर action होता है।

Example:
I update the website.

Subject: I
Verb: update
Object: the website

More examples:
- She checks the orders.
- Rahul fixed the issue.
- We tested the feature.

Practice:
इन subjects से sentences बनाएँ:
I, you, he, she, we, they


STEP 2: NOUNS
=============

Noun किसी person, place, thing, animal या idea का नाम होता है।

Examples:
- Person: developer, customer, Rahul
- Place: office, Delhi, website
- Thing: laptop, page, image
- Animal: dog, cat
- Idea: honesty, happiness, freedom

Types of nouns:

1. Common noun:
General name
- developer
- city
- website

2. Proper noun:
Specific name; capital letter से शुरू होता है।
- Rahul
- Delhi
- Shopify

3. Countable noun:
जिन्हें count कर सकते हैं।
- one page, two pages
- one image, five images

4. Uncountable noun:
जिन्हें सामान्य रूप से count नहीं करते।
- information
- advice
- water
- work

Common mistakes:
Incorrect: Please share an information.
Correct: Please share some information.

Incorrect: He gave me an advice.
Correct: He gave me some advice.
Correct: He gave me a piece of advice.

Practice:
अपने आसपास के 10 nouns लिखें और common/proper या countable/uncountable बताएँ।


STEP 3: PRONOUNS
================

Pronoun noun की जगह उपयोग होता है ताकि noun बार-बार repeat न हो।

Subject pronouns:
I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Object pronouns:
me, you, him, her, it, us, them

Possessive adjectives:
my, your, his, her, its, our, their

Possessive pronouns:
mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs

Examples:
- Rahul is a developer. He works on websites.
- I called Priya. I asked her about the issue.
- This is my laptop.
- This laptop is mine.

Common mistake:
Incorrect: Me updated the page.
Correct: I updated the page.

Practice:
नाम को pronoun से replace करें:
- Rahul checked the website.
- Priya sent the screenshots.
- The customers placed the orders.


STEP 4: ARTICLES — A, AN, THE
=============================

A:
एक non-specific singular noun के पहले, consonant sound के साथ।
- a page
- a website
- a user

An:
एक non-specific singular noun के पहले, vowel sound के साथ।
- an image
- an issue
- an hour

The:
किसी specific या पहले से known noun के पहले।
- the issue you reported
- the product page
- the changes

First mention:
- I found an issue.

Second mention:
- I fixed the issue.

Common mistakes:
Incorrect: I updated offers on product page.
Correct: I updated the offers on the product page.

Incorrect: I found a issue.
Correct: I found an issue.

Practice:
रिक्त स्थान में a, an या the लगाएँ:
1. I found ___ error.
2. Please check ___ error I reported.
3. She added ___ banner.
4. He completed the work in ___ hour.

Answers:
1. an
2. the
3. a
4. an


STEP 5: VERBS
=============

Verb action या state बताता है।

Action verbs:
work, update, fix, check, write, test

State verbs:
be, know, understand, like, need, believe

Verb forms:

V1: update
V2: updated
V3: updated
V-ing: updating

Irregular verb example:

V1: write
V2: wrote
V3: written
V-ing: writing

More irregular verbs:
go – went – gone
see – saw – seen
do – did – done
take – took – taken
give – gave – given
come – came – come

Practice:
रोज 5 verbs की चारों forms लिखें।


STEP 6: HELPING VERBS
=====================

Helping verbs main verb को tense, question या negative बनाने में सहायता करते हैं।

Be forms:
am, is, are, was, were

Have forms:
have, has, had

Do forms:
do, does, did

Future:
will

Examples:
- I am working.
- She is checking the page.
- They have completed the work.
- He does not understand the issue.
- Did you test the page?
- I will update it tomorrow.

Important:
Do/does/did के बाद V1 आता है।

Incorrect: Did you checked it?
Correct: Did you check it?


STEP 7: SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT
==============================

Simple Present में he, she, it या singular name के साथ verb में s/es लगता है।

- I work.
- You work.
- We work.
- They work.
- He works.
- She works.
- Rahul works.

Examples:
- The customer wants an update.
- The customers want an update.
- She checks the orders daily.
- They check the orders daily.

Common mistakes:
Incorrect: He work on websites.
Correct: He works on websites.

Incorrect: They works on websites.
Correct: They work on websites.


STEP 8: ADJECTIVES
==================

Adjective noun या pronoun की quality बताता है।

Examples:
- a fast website
- a beautiful design
- an important update
- a large image
- the mobile menu

Sentence examples:
- The website is fast.
- This is an important issue.
- She created a beautiful design.

Order:
Adjective सामान्य रूप से noun के पहले आता है।

Adjective + noun:
- a professional message
- a responsive layout

Be verb + adjective:
- The message is professional.
- The layout is responsive.

Comparison:

Positive:
fast

Comparative:
faster
- This website is faster than the old website.

Superlative:
fastest
- This is the fastest version.

More examples:
good – better – best
bad – worse – worst
easy – easier – easiest
beautiful – more beautiful – most beautiful

Common mistake:
Incorrect: This design is more better.
Correct: This design is better.


STEP 9: ADVERBS
================

Adverb verb, adjective या दूसरे adverb के बारे में अधिक जानकारी देता है।

Adverb of manner — action कैसे हुआ:
- quickly
- carefully
- clearly
- professionally

Examples:
- The page loads quickly.
- Please check the code carefully.
- Explain the issue clearly.

Adverb of frequency — action कितनी बार होता है:
- always
- usually
- often
- sometimes
- rarely
- never

Examples:
- I usually check the website in the morning.
- She always tests the mobile view.

Adverb of time:
- now
- today
- yesterday
- soon
- later

Adverb of degree:
- very
- quite
- too
- almost

Examples:
- The page is very slow.
- The image is too large.

Adjective vs adverb:
- She is a careful developer. (careful describes developer)
- She checks the code carefully. (carefully describes checks)

Common mistake:
Incorrect: The page loads quick.
Correct: The page loads quickly.


STEP 10: PREPOSITIONS
=====================

Prepositions time, place, position या direction का relation बताते हैं।

Time:

At:
- at 5 p.m.
- at night

On:
- on Monday
- on 28 July

In:
- in July
- in 2026
- in the morning

Place:

At:
- at the office
- at home

On:
- on the page
- on the website
- on the table

In:
- in the file
- in the folder
- in Delhi

Other useful prepositions:
- from the client
- to the customer
- with the team
- for two hours
- since Monday
- before the meeting
- after lunch
- between two sections

Common professional examples:
- I updated the offers on the product page.
- The code is in the file.
- I received a message from the client.
- Please share the details with the developer.


STEP 11: CONJUNCTIONS
=====================

Conjunctions words या sentences को जोड़ते हैं।

And:
दो similar ideas जोड़ता है।
- I checked the page and fixed the issue.

But:
Contrast दिखाता है।
- The design looks good, but the page is slow.

Or:
Choice दिखाता है।
- Please send a screenshot or a video.

Because:
Reason बताता है।
- The image is slow because the file is large.

So:
Result बताता है।
- The file was large, so I compressed it.

Although:
Unexpected contrast।
- Although the file is large, it loads quickly.

If:
Condition।
- If you approve the design, I will publish it.

===============

Tenses सीखने का सही क्रम:

1. Simple Present
2. Present Continuous
3. Simple Past
4. Simple Future
5. Present Perfect
6. Past Continuous
7. Present Perfect Continuous
8. Past Perfect
9. Future Continuous
10. Past Perfect Continuous
11. Future Perfect
12. Future Perfect Continuous

चार सबसे जरूरी tenses पहले सीखें:

Simple Present:
- I update the website every day.

Present Continuous:
- I am updating the website now.

Simple Past:
- I updated the website yesterday.

Simple Future:
- I will update the website tomorrow.

इसके बाद:

Present Perfect:

STEP 13: NEGATIVE SENTENCES AND QUESTIONS
==========================================

Simple Present:
- I work here.
- I do not work here.
- Do you work here?

With he/she/it:
- She works here.
- She does not work here.
- Does she work here?

Simple Past:
- I updated the page.
- I did not update the page.
- Did you update the page?

Present Continuous:
- I am checking the website.
- I am not checking the website.
- Are you checking the website?

Present Perfect:
- I have completed the work.
- I have not completed the work.
- Have you completed the work?

Future:
- I will update the page.
- I will not update the page.
- Will you update the page?


STEP 14: MODAL VERBS
====================

Modal + V1

Can:
Ability या permission
- I can fix the issue.
- Can I update the page?

Could:
Past ability या polite request
- Could you share a screenshot?

May:
Formal permission या possibility
- May I publish the changes?
- It may take some time.

Might:
Weak possibility
- The update might cause an issue.

Should:
Advice
- You should compress the image.

Must:
Strong necessity
- We must test the payment page.

Would:
Polite request या imagined situation
- Would you please review the changes?
- I would update it if I had access.

Common mistake:
Incorrect: I can fixed it.
Correct: I can fix it.


STEP 15: IMPERATIVES
====================

Imperative sentence instruction, request या command देता है।
Subject “you” समझा जाता है और लिखा नहीं जाता।

Examples:
- Check the website.
- Please review the changes.
- Send me a screenshot.
- Do not publish the page yet.

Polite forms:
- Please check the changes.
- Could you please check the changes?
- Please let me know if you need any revisions.


STEP 16: GERUNDS AND INFINITIVES
================================

Gerund:
Verb + ing noun की तरह उपयोग होता है।

- Testing is important.
- I enjoy learning English.
- Thank you for checking the page.

Infinitive:
To + V1

- I want to learn English.
- I need to update the page.
- She plans to test the website.

Common patterns:
- enjoy + ing
- avoid + ing
- finish + ing
- want + to + V1
- need + to + V1
- plan + to + V1

Incorrect: I enjoy to learn English.
Correct: I enjoy learning English.


STEP 17: ACTIVE AND PASSIVE VOICE
=================================

Active voice:
Subject action करता है।

- I updated the page.

Passive voice:
Action या result पर focus होता है।

- The page was updated by me.
- The issue has been resolved.

Structure:
Object + correct form of “be” + V3

Examples:
- I fixed the issue. (active)
- The issue was fixed. (passive)

- The developer has updated the website. (active)
- The website has been updated. (passive)

Professional messages में passive useful है:
- The requested changes have been completed.
- The issue has been resolved.


STEP 18: DIRECT AND INDIRECT SPEECH
===================================

Direct speech:
Exact words quotation marks में।

- The client said, “Please update the page.”

Indirect speech:
Meaning report किया जाता है।

- The client asked me to update the page.

More examples:
- He said, “I am busy.”
- He said that he was busy.

- She said, “I have completed the work.”
- She said that she had completed the work.


STEP 19: CONDITIONAL SENTENCES
==============================

Zero Conditional:
General truth

If + Simple Present, Simple Present
- If an image is large, it loads slowly.

First Conditional:
Real future possibility

If + Simple Present, will + V1
- If you approve the design, I will publish it.

Second Conditional:
Imaginary or unlikely present/future situation

If + Simple Past, would + V1
- If I had access, I would fix the issue.

Third Conditional:
Imaginary past situation

If + Past Perfect, would have + V3
- If I had known about the issue, I would have fixed it earlier.


STEP 20: CLAUSES AND COMPLEX SENTENCES
======================================

Independent clause:
अकेले complete sentence बन सकता है।

- I fixed the issue.

Dependent clause:
अकेले complete meaning नहीं देता।

- because the image was large

Combined sentence:
- I compressed the image because it was large.

Relative clauses:

Who — person:
- The developer who fixed the issue is available.

Which — thing:
- The image which you shared is too large.

That — person or thing:
- This is the page that I updated.

Where — place:
- This is the section where the error appears.


STEP 21: PUNCTUATION AND CAPITALIZATION
=======================================

Capital letter:
- Sentence के पहले word में
- Person, city, country और brand names में
- “I” हमेशा capital होता है

Correct:
- I work with Rahul in Delhi.

Full stop (.):
Statement के अंत में।
- I have completed the update.

Question mark (?):
Question के अंत में।
- Have you checked the update?

Comma (,):
List या clauses अलग करने के लिए।
- I checked the layout, images, buttons, and links.
- After testing the page, I published it.

Apostrophe (’):
Short forms या possession में।
- I’ve completed the work.
- Rahul’s laptop

Common mistake:
Incorrect: ive updated the page
Correct: I’ve updated the page.


STEP 22: PROFESSIONAL WRITING
=============================

Professional message structure:

1. बताइए कि आपने क्या किया।
2. User से क्या action चाहिए, वह बताइए।
3. Further help offer कीजिए।

Example:
I’ve updated the offers on the product page. Please review the changes and let me know if any further revisions are needed.

Reporting an issue:
I found an issue with the mobile menu. It does not display all menu items on smaller screens. I’m currently working on a fix.

Asking for details:
Could you please share a screenshot or screen recording of the issue? This will help me identify and resolve it more quickly.

Giving an update:
I’ve fixed the issue and tested the page on desktop and mobile devices. Please check it on your end and share your feedback.
