STORY 1: A NEW MORNING ROUTINE
==============================

Level: Beginner
Grammar focus: Simple Present

Ravi wakes up at six o’clock every morning. He drinks a glass of water and opens
the window. The fresh air makes him feel active. After that, he walks in the park
for twenty minutes.

When Ravi returns home, he takes a shower and eats breakfast. He usually eats
fruit, bread, and eggs. Before starting work, he reads five English words and
writes one sentence with each word.

Ravi does not study for many hours. He studies for only thirty minutes every day.
His daily practice slowly improves his English. He now understands simple
messages and speaks with more confidence.


Hindi summary:
--------------

रवि हर सुबह छह बजे उठता है। वह पानी पीता है, park में चलता है और breakfast
करता है। काम शुरू करने से पहले वह पाँच English words पढ़ता है और हर word से एक
sentence बनाता है। वह रोज केवल तीस minutes पढ़ता है, लेकिन regular practice से
उसकी English और confidence improve होते हैं।


Vocabulary:
-----------

Routine (रूटीन) — दिनचर्या
Meaning: Activities that you do regularly.
Example: Reading is part of my daily routine.

Wake up (वेक अप) — जागना
Meaning: To stop sleeping.
Example: I wake up at six o’clock.

Fresh (फ्रेश) — ताज़ा
Meaning: Clean, new, or full of energy.
Example: The morning air is fresh.

Return (रिटर्न) — वापस आना
Meaning: To come or go back.
Example: I return home at seven o’clock.

Practice (प्रैक्टिस) — अभ्यास
Meaning: Repeated work that improves a skill.
Example: Daily practice improves my English.

Slowly (स्लोली) — धीरे-धीरे
Meaning: At a low speed or over time.
Example: My vocabulary is slowly improving.

Confidence (कॉन्फिडन्स) — आत्मविश्वास
Meaning: Belief in your own ability.
Example: I speak English with confidence.


Grammar examples:
-----------------

Positive:
Ravi wakes up at six o’clock.

Negative:
Ravi does not study for many hours.

Question:
Does Ravi study English every day?


Questions:
----------

1. What time does Ravi wake up?
2. How long does he walk in the park?
3. What does he do before starting work?
4. How many English words does he read?
5. What improves his English?

STORY 2: THE MISSING FILE
=========================

Level: Beginner
Grammar focus: Simple Past

Meera worked on an important project last Monday. She completed the design and
saved the file on her computer. In the evening, her manager asked her to send the
final file.

Meera opened the project folder, but she could not find the file. She checked the
folder again and searched her entire computer. She became worried because the
manager was waiting.

Then Meera remembered that she had changed the file name. She searched for the
new name and immediately found it. She reviewed the design once more and sent it
to her manager.

The manager liked the design and thanked her. Meera learned an important lesson:
always use clear file names and keep project files organised.


Hindi summary:
--------------

मीरा ने एक important design complete करके computer में save किया। बाद में उसे
file नहीं मिली क्योंकि उसने उसका नाम बदल दिया था। नया नाम याद आने पर उसने file
ढूँढी और manager को भेज दी। उसने सीखा कि files के clear names रखने चाहिए।


Vocabulary:
-----------

Important (इम्पॉर्टन्ट) — महत्वपूर्ण
Example: This is an important project.

Complete (कम्प्लीट) — पूरा करना
Example: I completed the design yesterday.

Entire (एन्टायर) — पूरा
Example: She searched the entire computer.

Worried (वरिड) — चिंतित
Example: I was worried about the missing file.

Immediately (इमीडिएटली) — तुरंत
Example: She immediately found the file.

Review (रिव्यू) — दोबारा जाँच करना
Example: Please review the changes.

Organised (ऑर्गनाइज़्ड) — व्यवस्थित
Example: Keep your project folders organised.


Verb forms:
-----------

V1       V2          V3
work     worked      worked
complete completed   completed
save     saved       saved
find     found       found
become   became      become
send     sent        sent
learn    learned     learned


Questions:
----------

1. When did Meera work on the project?
2. Why could she not find the file?
3. How did she finally find it?
4. What did the manager think about the design?
5. What lesson did Meera learn?


Writing practice:
-----------------

Use these words:
found, checked, fixed, completed, learned


STORY 3: A CLIENT REPORTS AN ISSUE
==================================

Level: Elementary
Grammar focus: Present Continuous and professional communication

Arjun is working as a website developer. One afternoon, a client sends him a
message: “The mobile menu is not showing all the links. Please check it.”

Arjun first opens the website on his phone. The menu works correctly on his
device, so he asks the client for a screenshot and the phone model. The client
sends a short screen recording.

After watching the recording, Arjun understands the problem. A large image is
covering part of the menu on smaller screens. He changes the image position and
tests the menu on different screen sizes.

Arjun then writes:

“I’ve fixed the mobile menu and tested it on different screen sizes. Please
review the changes and let me know if you experience any further issues.”

The client checks the website and confirms that the menu is working properly.


Hindi summary:
--------------

Client ने mobile menu की problem बताई। अर्जुन के phone पर problem नहीं थी, इसलिए
उसने screenshot और screen recording माँगी। Recording देखकर उसने पाया कि एक बड़ी
image menu को cover कर रही थी। उसने issue fix किया, अलग screen sizes पर test किया
और client को professional update भेजा।


Vocabulary:
-----------

Report (रिपोर्ट) — किसी समस्या की जानकारी देना
Example: The client reported an issue.

Device (डिवाइस) — electronic उपकरण
Example: The website works on my device.

Screenshot (स्क्रीनशॉट) — screen की तस्वीर
Example: Please share a screenshot.

Recording (रिकॉर्डिंग) — record किया हुआ audio या video
Example: The client sent a screen recording.

Cover (कवर) — ढकना
Example: The image is covering the menu.

Different (डिफरन्ट) — अलग-अलग
Example: Test the website on different devices.

Further (फर्दर) — अतिरिक्त / आगे की
Example: Let me know if further changes are needed.

Properly (प्रॉपरली) — सही तरीके से
Example: The menu is working properly.


Useful professional sentences:
------------------------------

- Could you please share a screenshot of the issue?
- I’m checking the problem on different devices.
- I’ve identified the cause of the issue.
- I’ve fixed and tested the mobile menu.
- Please review the changes on your end.


Questions:
----------

1. What problem did the client report?
2. Why did Arjun ask for a screen recording?
3. What was covering the menu?
4. How did Arjun test his fix?
5. What did the client confirm?


Speaking practice:
------------------

Imagine that a client reports a slow-loading image.
Explain what you will check and how you will reply.


STORY 4: FIVE WORDS EVERY DAY
=============================

Level: Elementary
Grammar focus: Present Perfect

Sonia has wanted to improve her English for a long time. In the past, she tried
to learn fifty words in one day. She quickly forgot most of them and stopped
studying after a few days.

This month, Sonia has started a simpler method. She learns only five words every
day. She writes the pronunciation, Hindi meaning, and one example sentence for
each word. She also reviews yesterday’s words before learning new ones.

Sonia has followed this routine for three weeks. She has learned more than one
hundred words and has used many of them in conversations. More importantly, she
has not stopped practising.

Sonia now knows that a small habit can create a big improvement when it is
repeated consistently.


Hindi summary:
--------------

सोनिया पहले एक दिन में बहुत सारे words सीखने की कोशिश करती थी और जल्दी भूल जाती
थी। अब वह रोज केवल पाँच words pronunciation, meaning और example के साथ सीखती है।
तीन weeks में उसने सौ से अधिक words सीख लिए हैं और उसकी practice लगातार चल रही है।


Vocabulary:
-----------

Method (मेथड) — तरीका
Example: This is a simple learning method.

Pronunciation (प्रनन्सिएशन) — शब्द बोलने का तरीका
Example: Check the pronunciation of new words.

Review (रिव्यू) — दोबारा पढ़ना या जाँचना
Example: Review yesterday’s vocabulary.

Conversation (कन्वर्सेशन) — बातचीत
Example: Use new words in conversation.

Habit (हैबिट) — आदत
Example: Reading every day is a good habit.

Improvement (इम्प्रूवमेंट) — सुधार
Example: I can see an improvement in my English.

Consistently (कन्सिस्टन्टली) — नियमित रूप से
Example: Practise consistently to improve.


Present Perfect examples:
-------------------------

- Sonia has started a new method.
- She has learned more than one hundred words.
- She has used new words in conversations.
- She has not stopped practising.


Questions:
----------

1. What did Sonia try to do in the past?
2. Why did her old method fail?
3. How many words does she learn every day now?
4. How long has she followed the new routine?
5. What has she learned about small habits?

STORY 5: THE COURAGE TO SPEAK
=============================

Level: Intermediate
Grammar focus: Past Continuous and confidence

Kabir understood basic English, but he was afraid of speaking. He worried that
people would laugh when he made a mistake. Because of this fear, he remained
silent during meetings.

One day, Kabir was attending an online meeting with an international client. His
manager was explaining the project when the internet connection suddenly
stopped. The client asked a question, but nobody answered.

Kabir knew the answer. His hands were shaking, but he turned on his microphone.
He spoke slowly and used simple words. His sentence was not perfect, but the
client understood him and thanked him for the clear explanation.

After the meeting, Kabir’s manager said, “Good communication does not require
perfect English. It requires clear thinking and the courage to speak.”

That experience changed Kabir’s attitude. He started speaking a little English
every day. He still made mistakes, but he no longer allowed fear to control him.


Hindi summary:
--------------

कबीर English समझता था लेकिन mistake के डर से बोलता नहीं था। एक meeting में
manager का internet बंद हो गया और client ने question पूछा। कबीर ने डर के बावजूद
simple English में जवाब दिया। Client ने उसे समझा और धन्यवाद दिया। कबीर ने सीखा
कि communication के लिए perfect English नहीं, clarity और courage जरूरी हैं।


Vocabulary:
-----------

Afraid (अफ्रेड) — डरा हुआ
Example: Do not be afraid of mistakes.

Fear (फियर) — डर
Example: Fear can stop us from speaking.

Remain (रिमेन) — बने रहना
Example: He remained silent.

Suddenly (सडनली) — अचानक
Example: The internet suddenly stopped.

Shake (शेक) — काँपना
Example: His hands were shaking.

Courage (करिज) — साहस
Example: It takes courage to speak.

Attitude (ऐटिट्यूड) — सोचने का तरीका / दृष्टिकोण
Example: A positive attitude helps us learn.

Allow (अलाउ) — अनुमति देना
Example: Do not allow fear to control you.


Grammar examples:
-----------------

Past Continuous:
- Kabir was attending an online meeting.
- His manager was explaining the project.
- His hands were shaking.

Simple Past:
- The internet connection stopped.
- The client asked a question.
- Kabir turned on his microphone.


Questions:
----------

1. Why was Kabir afraid of speaking English?
2. What happened during the online meeting?
3. How did Kabir answer the client?
4. What advice did his manager give him?
5. How did the experience change Kabir?


Speaking challenge:
-------------------

Read this sentence five times:

to speak.”

Now speak for one minute about a task you completed recently.


STORY 6: THE SMALL SHOP THAT WENT ONLINE
========================================

Level: Intermediate
Grammar focus: Sequence of events and business vocabulary

Mr. Sharma owned a small clothing shop in Jaipur. For many years, customers
visited his shop to buy traditional sarees and handmade garments. The shop was
popular in the local market, but people from other cities could not easily buy
its products.

His daughter, Ananya, suggested creating an online store. At first, Mr. Sharma
was uncertain. He believed that building a website would be expensive and
difficult to manage. Ananya explained that they could begin with a small
collection and gradually add more products.

They photographed twenty popular products, wrote clear descriptions, and added
prices and delivery information. Ananya also tested the website on desktop and
mobile devices before launching it.

During the first week, the online store received only two orders. Mr. Sharma felt
disappointed, but Ananya continued sharing product photos and customer reviews on
social media. After three months, orders were arriving from several cities.

Mr. Sharma realised that success did not come from simply launching the website.
It came from improving the store, listening to customers, and working
consistently.


Hindi summary:
--------------

शर्मा जी की Jaipur में clothing shop थी। उनकी बेटी अनन्या ने online store बनाने
का सुझाव दिया। शुरुआत में कम orders आए, लेकिन regular product sharing, customer
reviews और continuous improvement से तीन months बाद कई cities से orders आने लगे।


Business vocabulary:
--------------------

Own (ओन) — मालिक होना
Example: Mr. Sharma owns a clothing shop.

Traditional (ट्रडिशनल) — पारंपरिक
Example: The shop sells traditional sarees.

Suggest (सजेस्ट) — सुझाव देना
Example: Ananya suggested creating a website.

Uncertain (अनसर्टन) — अनिश्चित
Example: He was uncertain about the idea.

Gradually (ग्रैजुअली) — धीरे-धीरे
Example: They gradually added more products.

Description (डिस्क्रिप्शन) — विवरण
Example: Write a clear product description.

Launch (लॉन्च) — शुरू या प्रकाशित करना
Example: They launched the online store.

Disappointed (डिसअपॉइन्टिड) — निराश
Example: He felt disappointed by the low sales.

Realise (रियलाइज़) — समझ में आना
Example: He realised that success takes time.

Consistently (कन्सिस्टन्टली) — लगातार
Example: They worked consistently.


Questions:
----------

1. What did Mr. Sharma sell?
2. Why did Ananya suggest an online store?
3. Why was Mr. Sharma uncertain?
4. How many products did they add first?
5. What helped the store receive more orders?

-----------------
