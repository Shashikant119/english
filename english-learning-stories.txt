STORY 1: THE FIRST ENGLISH MESSAGE
Level: Beginner
==================================

Ravi worked as a website developer. He was good at fixing websites, but he was not confident about writing English messages.

One morning, a client asked him to update a product page. Ravi completed the update in thirty minutes. He wanted to inform the client, but he did not know how to write the message correctly.

First, he wrote:

“I updated product page. Check and tell me.”

Ravi read the sentence again. He understood that it did not sound polite or professional. He opened his English notes and found a better sentence:

“I’ve updated the product page. Please review the changes and let me know if any further updates are needed.”

He sent the new message to the client. A few minutes later, the client replied, “Thank you. The page looks good.”

Ravi felt happy and confident. He learned that a clear message does not need difficult words. It only needs correct grammar, polite language, and a simple structure.


HINDI SUMMARY
-------------

Ravi एक website developer था। वह websites ठीक करने में अच्छा था, लेकिन English messages लिखने में confident नहीं था। उसने client का product page update किया और पहले एक incorrect message लिखा। अपने English notes की सहायता से उसने message को polite और professional बनाया। Client ने उसके काम की प्रशंसा की। Ravi ने सीखा कि अच्छा message लिखने के लिए कठिन words नहीं, बल्कि correct grammar और clear structure चाहिए।


VOCABULARY
----------

1. Developer (डिवेलपर) — वेबसाइट या software बनाने वाला व्यक्ति
   Example: Ravi is a website developer.

2. Confident (कॉन्फिडन्ट) — आत्मविश्वासी
   Example: I feel confident when I practice.

3. Update (अपडेट) — बदलाव करना / नई जानकारी देना
   Example: I updated the product page.

4. Inform (इन्फॉर्म) — जानकारी देना
   Example: Please inform the client about the changes.

5. Correctly (करेक्टली) — सही तरीके से
   Example: She answered the question correctly.

6. Polite (पलाइट) — विनम्र
   Example: Always use polite language with clients.

7. Professional (प्रफेशनल) — कार्य-संबंधी और उचित
   Example: He wrote a professional message.

8. Review (रिव्यू) — ध्यान से जाँच करना
   Example: Please review the changes.

9. Reply (रिप्लाइ) — जवाब देना
   Example: The client replied quickly.

10. Structure (स्ट्रक्चर) — बनावट / क्रम
    Example: This sentence has a clear structure.


GRAMMAR POINT
-------------

Present Perfect:
“I’ve updated the product page.”

I’ve = I have

यह tense recently completed work के लिए उपयोग होता है, जिसका result अभी relevant है।

Structure:
Subject + has/have + V3

More examples:
- I have completed the work.
- She has fixed the issue.
- We have tested the website.


COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. What was Ravi’s profession?
2. Why was Ravi not confident?
3. What did the client ask him to do?
4. What was wrong with Ravi’s first message?
5. What did Ravi learn at the end?

Suggested answers:

1. Ravi was a website developer.
2. He was not confident about writing English messages.
3. The client asked him to update a product page.
4. It was not polite or professional.
5. He learned to use correct grammar, polite language, and a simple structure.


SPEAKING PRACTICE
-----------------

STORY 2: FIVE WORDS EVERY DAY
Level: Beginner
=============================

Meera wanted to improve her English vocabulary. She bought a large notebook and decided to learn fifty words every day.

On the first day, she wrote fifty new words. She tried to remember all of them, but the next morning she could remember only six. She felt disappointed.

Her teacher said, “Do not try to learn too many words at once. Learn five useful words every day. Read them, pronounce them, write them, and use them in sentences.”

Meera followed the advice. Her first five words were:

improve, practice, explain, receive, and complete.

She wrote one sentence for each word:

“I want to improve my English.”
“I practice speaking every morning.”
“Please explain this rule.”
“I received your message.”
“I have completed the task.”

Every evening, she revised the old words before learning new ones. After one month, she knew more than one hundred useful words and could use them correctly.

Meera discovered that learning slowly was not a weakness. It was a smart and effective method.


HINDI SUMMARY
-------------

Meera रोज पचास words सीखना चाहती थी, लेकिन वह उन्हें याद नहीं रख पाई। Teacher ने उसे रोज केवल पाँच useful words सीखने और उनसे sentences बनाने की सलाह दी। Meera ने पुराने words revise किए और एक महीने में सौ से अधिक useful words सीख लिए। उसने समझा कि धीरे-धीरे सीखना एक effective method है।


VOCABULARY
----------

1. Vocabulary (वोकैब्यलरी) — शब्दावली
   Example: Reading improves your vocabulary.

2. Decide (डिसाइड) — निर्णय लेना
   Example: I decided to learn English.

3. Remember (रिमेम्बर) — याद रखना
   Example: I cannot remember this word.

4. Disappointed (डिसअपॉइन्टिड) — निराश
   Example: She felt disappointed after the test.

5. Advice (अडवाइस) — सलाह
   Example: My teacher gave me useful advice.

6. Follow (फॉलो) — अनुसरण करना
   Example: Please follow these instructions.

7. Revise (रिवाइज़) — दोबारा पढ़ना
   Example: Revise the old words every day.

8. Discover (डिस्कवर) — पता लगाना
   Example: I discovered a better learning method.

9. Effective (इफेक्टिव) — प्रभावी
   Example: Daily practice is an effective method.

10. Weakness (वीकनस) — कमजोरी
    Example: Making mistakes is not a weakness.


GRAMMAR POINT
-------------

Simple Past:

- She bought a notebook.
- She wrote fifty words.
- Her teacher gave her advice.

Simple Past completed past actions के लिए उपयोग होता है।

Structure:
Subject + V2 + object

Negative:
Subject + did not + V1

Example:
She did not remember all the words.

Question:
Did + subject + V1?

Example:
Did she follow the advice?


COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. How many words did Meera initially try to learn?
2. How many words could she remember the next morning?
3. What advice did her teacher give?
4. What did Meera do every evening?
5. Why was her new method effective?

STORY 3: THE LARGE IMAGE
Level: Elementary
========================

Amit was working on an online store. The owner reported that the product page was loading very slowly on mobile phones.

Amit checked the code, but he did not find any major error. Then he tested the page on a slow internet connection. He noticed that a product image was more than eight megabytes in size.

The image looked beautiful, but it was too large for a website. Amit compressed it and created different image sizes for desktop and mobile devices. He also enabled lazy loading for images that were not immediately visible.

After making the changes, he tested the page again. It opened much faster than before. The owner was pleased with the result.

Amit sent a message:

“I found that a large product image was slowing down the page. I’ve optimized the image and enabled lazy loading. The page now loads faster on mobile devices.”

This experience taught Amit an important lesson: before changing complex code, always check the simple causes of a problem.


HINDI SUMMARY
-------------

Online store का product page mobile पर slow था। Amit को code में कोई बड़ी error नहीं मिली। बाद में उसने पाया कि product image आठ megabytes से बड़ी थी। उसने image compress की, अलग-अलग sizes बनाईं और lazy loading enable की। इसके बाद page तेजी से load होने लगा। उसने सीखा कि complex code बदलने से पहले problem के simple causes check करने चाहिए।


VOCABULARY
----------

1. Report (रिपोर्ट) — किसी समस्या की जानकारी देना
2. Connection (कनेक्शन) — जुड़ाव / internet connection
3. Notice (नोटिस) — ध्यान देना
4. Megabyte (मेगाबाइट) — digital file size की unit
5. Compress (कम्प्रेस) — file size कम करना
6. Device (डिवाइस) — electronic उपकरण
7. Enable (इनेबल) — चालू करना
8. Immediately (इमीडिएटली) — तुरंत
9. Optimize (ऑप्टिमाइज़) — अधिक efficient बनाना
10. Complex (कॉम्प्लेक्स) — जटिल
11. Cause (कॉज़) — कारण
12. Experience (इक्सपीरिअन्स) — अनुभव


GRAMMAR POINT
-------------

Comparative adjectives:

- The page is faster than before.
- This image is smaller than the old image.
- Mobile performance is better now.

Common forms:
fast – faster – fastest
small – smaller – smallest
large – larger – largest
good – better – best
bad – worse – worst

Incorrect:
The page is more faster.

Correct:
The page is faster.


COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. What problem did the store owner report?
2. How large was the product image?
3. What three changes did Amit make?
4. What happened after the changes?
5. What lesson did Amit learn?

STORY 4: A QUESTION SAVED THE PROJECT
Level: Elementary
=====================================

Sara received a message from a client:

“Please change the homepage design.”

The request was not clear. Sara could have started changing everything, but she decided to ask a few questions first.

She replied:

“Could you please explain which sections you would like to change? It would also be helpful if you could share a reference design or screenshot.”

The client sent a screenshot and explained that only the banner and product-card colors needed to be changed. The rest of the homepage was already correct.

Because Sara asked for clarification, she completed the task in two hours. Without clarification, she might have spent the entire day changing sections that did not need any work.

The client appreciated her careful approach. Sara learned that asking a clear question is not a sign of confusion. It is an important professional skill.


HINDI SUMMARY
-------------

Client ने homepage design बदलने के लिए कहा, लेकिन request clear नहीं थी। Sara ने काम शुरू करने से पहले questions पूछे और reference screenshot माँगा। Client ने बताया कि केवल banner और product-card colors बदलने थे। Clarification के कारण Sara ने task जल्दी complete किया और unnecessary work से बच गई।


VOCABULARY
----------

1. Request (रिक्वेस्ट) — अनुरोध
2. Reference (रेफरन्स) — उदाहरण के रूप में उपयोग की गई चीज
3. Clarification (क्लैरिफिकेशन) — बात को स्पष्ट करना
4. Entire (एन्टायर) — पूरा
5. Appreciate (अप्रिशिएट) — सराहना करना
6. Approach (अप्रोच) — काम करने का तरीका
7. Confusion (कन्फ्यूज़न) — उलझन
8. Skill (स्किल) — कौशल
9. Helpful (हेल्पफुल) — सहायक
10. Unnecessary (अननेसेसरी) — जिसकी जरूरत न हो


GRAMMAR POINT
-------------

Polite questions with could:

Direct:
Explain the changes.

Polite:
Could you please explain the changes?

Direct:
Send a screenshot.

Polite:
Could you please send a screenshot?

Useful structures:
- Could you please share more details?
- Could you explain where the issue appears?
- Would you please review the changes?
- It would be helpful if you could share an example.


COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. Why was the client’s first message unclear?
2. What did Sara ask the client to share?
3. Which parts of the homepage needed changes?
4. How much time did Sara take to complete the task?
5. Why is asking for clarification important?


SPEAKING PRACTICE
-----------------

Say these sentences aloud:

- Could you please explain the issue?
- Could you share a screenshot?
- Which section would you like me to update?
- Please let me know the exact changes you need.


STORY 5: THIRTY MINUTES OF PRACTICE
Level: Pre-intermediate
===================================

Arjun often said, “I do not have enough time to learn English.” He worked for nine hours every day and felt tired in the evening.

One day, he calculated how much time he spent watching short videos on his phone. He was surprised to discover that he spent almost two hours a day on them.

Arjun decided to use thirty minutes of that time for English:

- Ten minutes for reading
- Five minutes for pronunciation
- Five minutes for vocabulary
- Ten minutes for writing and speaking

During the first week, the routine felt difficult. Arjun frequently forgot words and made grammar mistakes. However, he did not stop. He wrote every corrected sentence in a notebook and revised it the next day.

After three months, he could read emails more easily, understand client messages, and write clear replies without translating every sentence from Hindi.

Arjun did not become fluent in one day. He improved because he practiced consistently. He learned that progress is usually quiet and slow, but it becomes visible when we continue working.


HINDI SUMMARY
-------------

Arjun सोचता था कि उसके पास English सीखने का समय नहीं है। उसने देखा कि वह रोज दो घंटे short videos देखता था। उसने उसमें से तीस minutes English practice के लिए उपयोग किए। शुरुआत में mistakes हुईं, लेकिन उसने corrected sentences लिखे और revise किए। तीन महीने बाद उसकी reading और writing बेहतर हो गई। उसने सीखा कि consistent practice से progress होती है।


VOCABULARY
----------

1. Calculate (कैल्क्युलेट) — गणना करना
2. Surprised (सरप्राइज़्ड) — आश्चर्यचकित
3. Almost (ऑलमोस्ट) — लगभग
4. Routine (रूटीन) — नियमित दिनचर्या
5. Frequently (फ्रीक्वेन्टली) — अक्सर
6. Corrected (करेक्टिड) — सुधारा हुआ
7. Translate (ट्रान्सलेट) — अनुवाद करना
8. Fluent (फ्लूअन्ट) — आसानी और प्रवाह से बोलने वाला
9. Consistently (कन्सिस्टन्टली) — नियमित रूप से
10. Progress (प्रोग्रेस) — प्रगति
11. Visible (विज़िबल) — दिखाई देने वाला
12. Continue (कन्टिन्यू) — जारी रखना


GRAMMAR POINT
-------------

Adjective and adverb:

Adjective noun की quality बताता है:
- It was a difficult routine.
- He wrote a clear reply.
- His progress was slow.

Adverb action के बारे में बताता है:
- He practiced consistently.
- He read the email easily.
- He replied clearly.

Compare:
- She is a careful developer. (adjective)
- She checks the code carefully. (adverb)


COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. Why did Arjun think he could not learn English?
2. How much time did he spend watching short videos?
3. How did he divide his thirty-minute practice?
4. What did he do with corrected sentences?
5. What improvements did he notice after three months?

STORY 6: THE HONEST UPDATE
Level: Pre-intermediate
===========================

Neha promised to complete a website update by Friday evening. On Friday afternoon, she discovered an unexpected technical problem. If she continued working quickly without testing, the checkout page might stop working.

Neha had two choices. She could remain silent and miss the deadline, or she could explain the situation honestly.

She sent this message:

“I’ve completed most of the requested changes. However, I found an unexpected issue during testing. I need some additional time to resolve it safely. I will complete and retest the update by Saturday afternoon.”

The client appreciated the early warning and approved the new deadline. Neha fixed the problem, tested the checkout carefully, and delivered the update on Saturday morning—earlier than her revised deadline.

The situation taught her that professional communication is not only about sharing good news. It is also about reporting problems early, explaining their impact, and giving a realistic solution.


HINDI SUMMARY
-------------

Neha ने Friday तक website update complete करने का promise किया था। Testing के दौरान उसे unexpected technical problem मिली। उसने problem छिपाने के बजाय client को honest update दिया, impact समझाया और realistic deadline दी। Client ने नई deadline approve कर दी। Neha ने problem safely fix करके काम समय से पहले deliver कर दिया।


VOCABULARY
----------

1. Promise (प्रॉमिस) — वादा करना
2. Unexpected (अनइक्सपेक्टिड) — जिसकी उम्मीद न हो
3. Technical (टेक्निकल) — तकनीकी
4. Deadline (डेडलाइन) — काम पूरा करने की अंतिम तारीख
5. Remain (रिमेन) — बने रहना
6. Honestly (ऑनिस्टली) — ईमानदारी से
7. Additional (अडिशनल) — अतिरिक्त
8. Resolve (रिज़ॉल्व) — समाधान करना
9. Approve (अप्रूव) — स्वीकृति देना
10. Deliver (डिलिवर) — काम सौंपना
11. Impact (इम्पैक्ट) — प्रभाव
12. Realistic (रियलिस्टिक) — वास्तविक स्थिति के अनुसार


GRAMMAR POINT
-------------

However:
दो contrasting ideas को जोड़ता है।

- I completed most changes. However, I found an issue.
- The task was difficult. However, she completed it.

Because:
Reason बताता है।

- I need more time because I found an issue.

So:
Result बताता है।

- I found an issue, so I need more time.

If:
Condition बताता है।

- If I rush the update, the checkout might stop working.


COMPREHENSION QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. When did Neha promise to complete the update?
2. What problem did she discover?
3. What two choices did she have?
4. How did the client respond?
5. What are the three parts of good problem reporting?
