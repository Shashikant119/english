1. I’ve tested it on my end. Please check it and let me know what issue you’re experiencing. I’ll make the necessary updates accordingly.
2. Sir, I’ve manually updated the collections. Please review them and let me know if any further changes are needed.
3. I’ve made the update. Please check it and let me know if any further changes are needed.
4. I’ve updated the offers on the product page. Please review them and let me know if any further changes are needed.

Grammar explanation:
- “I’ve” is the short form of “I have.”
- “I have updated” is in the present perfect tense. It shows that the work was completed recently and the result is relevant now.
- We use “the offers” because we are referring to specific offers.
- We use “the product page” because we are referring to a specific page.

Incorrect: I’ve updated offers on product page.
Correct: I’ve updated the offers on the product page.

Examples:
- I’ve updated the product images on the collection page.
- I’ve added the discount information to the product page.
- I’ve fixed the layout on the mobile product page.
