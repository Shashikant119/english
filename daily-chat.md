> Hey! I'm fine. Today, I woke up early, went to the bathroom, took a bath, and

### Talking about work and hobbies

**Original**

> I created a feature that sorts products from newest to oldest. I love coding
> and gyming and swiming, every early I'm going to gym.

**Corrected**

> I created a feature that sorts products from newest to oldest. I love coding,
> working out, and swimming. I go to the gym early every morning.

**Corrections**

- `gyming` → `working out` or `going to the gym`
- `swiming` → `swimming`
- `every early` → `early every morning`
- `I'm going to the gym` → `I go to the gym` when describing a regular habit

- **working out** — exercising
- **sorting feature** — functionality that arranges items in a particular order
- **newest to oldest** — descending order based on date
- **regular habit** — something you do repeatedly

### Personal English improvement routine

1. Speak in English for 10 minutes every day.
2. Write five sentences about your day.
3. Read the corrected sentences in this file aloud.
4. Learn five useful words and use each word in a sentence.
5. Listen to a short English video and repeat the speaker's sentences.
6. Practice common work conversations about bugs, clients, meetings, and
   project updates.
7. Review common verb forms such as `wake/woke`, `go/went`, `eat/ate`, and
   `start/started`.
8. Record your voice once a week and listen for pronunciation mistakes.

### Describing completed client work

**Original**

> I resolved newest to oldest sorting an collection page. and I sent message in
> english to cleint.

> message in English to the client.
