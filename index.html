Your daily 30-minute plan

Small, consistent practice is more effective than studying many rules in one day.

  1. Read and pronounce 5 new words.
  2. Study one grammar rule with examples.
  3. Write 5 sentences related to your work.
  4. Convert 2 sentences into negatives and questions.
  5. Read all sentences aloud and correct mistakes.
