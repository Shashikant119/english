# Daily 15-Minute English Practice

Use this short routine every day. Do not try to learn everything at once.

## 1. Read aloud — 3 minutes

Read these sentences slowly three times:

1. I am learning English every day.
   - Hindi: मैं हर दिन अंग्रेज़ी सीख रहा हूँ।

2. I am not afraid of making mistakes.
   - Hindi: मुझे गलतियाँ करने से डर नहीं लगता।

3. Please speak a little more slowly.
   - Hindi: कृपया थोड़ा धीरे बोलिए।

4. Could you please explain this to me?
   - Hindi: क्या आप कृपया मुझे यह समझा सकते हैं?

5. I will check the issue and update you.
   - Hindi: मैं समस्या जाँचकर आपको जानकारी दूँगा।

## 2. Speak about your day — 4 minutes

Complete these sentences aloud:

- I woke up at ______ today.
- I reached the office at ______.
- Today, I am working on ______.
- I fixed ______.
- After work, I will ______.

Example:

> I woke up at seven today. I reached the office at ten. Today, I am working
> on a Shopify theme. I fixed a product-image issue. After work, I will go to
> the gym.

## 3. Learn five words — 3 minutes

1. **Available** — उपलब्ध
   - I will be available tomorrow.

2. **Arrange** — व्यवस्था करना
   - I will arrange everything for the shoot.

3. **Coordinate** — तालमेल बनाना
   - Please coordinate with the team.

4. **Resolve** — समस्या हल करना
   - I resolved the website issue.

5. **Improve** — सुधारना
   - I want to improve my English.

## 4. Understand a work message — 2 minutes

Original message:

> We'll be doing the KOR lifestyle shoot tomorrow in the studio and office
> from 11 AM to 1 PM. Please coordinate accordingly.

Simple Hindi meaning:

> कल 11 बजे से 1 बजे तक स्टूडियो और ऑफिस में KOR का लाइफस्टाइल फोटोशूट होगा।
> सभी लोग उसी हिसाब से तैयारी और तालमेल रखें।

Useful patterns:

- **We'll be doing...** — हम ... करेंगे।
- **Tomorrow from 11 AM to 1 PM** — कल सुबह 11 से दोपहर 1 बजे तक।
- **Please coordinate accordingly.** — कृपया उसी हिसाब से तालमेल रखें।

## 5. Write three sentences — 3 minutes

Write one sentence for each topic:

1. What did I do today?
2. What am I doing now?
3. What will I do tomorrow?

Example:

- I fixed a website issue today.
- I am practising English now.
- I will attend a meeting tomorrow.

## Daily rule

- Speak even if your English is not perfect.
- Use short sentences first.
- Read every corrected sentence aloud.
- Learn five words, but use at least two in your own sentences.
- Practise for 15 minutes every day instead of studying for hours once a week.
